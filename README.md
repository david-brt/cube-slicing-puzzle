# Cube Slicing Puzzle

An interactive 3×3×3 cube slicing puzzle. Cut the cube with one plane at a time,
rearrange the pieces between cuts, and try to reach 27 unit cubes in as few cuts as possible.

**Try it out: https://david-brt.github.io/cube-slicing-puzzle/**

AI generated for demonstration purposes. Inspired by [3B1B's video on a 2025 IMO problem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nbwv5wHQoj0&t=1425s).
